About

I am a PhD student in the School of Psychology at South China Normal University, advised by Prof. Xianyou He.

My research focuses on how visual cues shape social perception, moral judgment, and aesthetic experience. I am interested in how people use appearance cues such as facial features, body shape, and clothing to form impressions of others. I examine how presenting images as art influences moral judgments. I also explore why people enjoy ambiguity in art. I combine behavioral experiments, eye tracking, and fMRI to investigate these questions.
